The Graph Network launched on Ethereum Mainnet in 2020. To lower gas costs for everyone who uses the protocol, The Graph moved to Arbitrum One, an Ethereum Layer 2. Today all protocol activity happens on Arbitrum One, including staking, delegation, curation, indexing rewards, and Subgraph Studio billing.

GRT still exists on both networks. If you hold GRT on Ethereum Mainnet, you need to bridge it to Arbitrum One before you can use it on The Graph. This page applies to every role: Delegators, Indexers, Subgraph developers, and token holders.

## GRT on Arbitrum One vs. Ethereum Mainnet

|  | Arbitrum One | Ethereum Mainnet |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Used by The Graph protocol | Yes | No |
| Pay for queries in Subgraph Studio | Yes | Studio can bridge it for you during deposit (see below) |
| Stake, delegate, or curate | Yes | No |
| Gas paid in | ETH on Arbitrum One | ETH on Ethereum Mainnet |
| GRT token contract | [0x9623063377AD1B27544C965cCd7342f7EA7e88C7](https://arbiscan.io/token/0x9623063377ad1b27544c965ccd7342f7ea7e88c7) | [0xc944E90C64B2c07662A292be6244BDf05Cda44a7](https://etherscan.io/token/0xc944e90c64b2c07662a292be6244bdf05cda44a7) |

> If you don't have any GRT yet, see [Getting GRT](/resources/getting-grt/). Some exchanges let you withdraw GRT directly to Arbitrum One, which means you won't need to bridge at all.

## How to Bridge GRT from Ethereum to Arbitrum One

You will need ETH on Ethereum Mainnet to pay gas for the bridging transaction, and ETH on Arbitrum One to use your GRT once it arrives. See [Getting Ether](/resources/getting-grt/#getting-ether).

Choose one of these options:

- **The Arbitrum Bridge:** The official bridge at [bridge.arbitrum.io](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/?l2ChainId=42161). Connect your wallet, choose Ethereum as the source and Arbitrum One as the destination, select GRT, enter an amount, and confirm. Deposits typically arrive on Arbitrum One within about 15 minutes.
- **A third-party bridge or aggregator:** Services such as [TransferTo](https://transferto.xyz/swap) can route GRT between networks. Check fees and the destination token contract before you confirm.
- **Subgraph Studio (billing only):** When you add GRT to your Subgraph Studio billing balance, you can deposit GRT from Ethereum Mainnet and Studio bridges it to Arbitrum One for you. It takes about 15-20 minutes to process. This option funds your billing balance only, not your wallet. See [Adding GRT using a wallet](/subgraphs/providers/subgraph-studio/billing/#adding-grt-using-a-wallet).

Once the bridge completes, add the Arbitrum One GRT contract address above to your wallet to see your balance.

## How to Bridge GRT from Arbitrum One back to Ethereum

You can move GRT back to Ethereum Mainnet with the [Arbitrum Bridge](https://bridge.arbitrum.io/?l2ChainId=42161) by choosing Arbitrum One as the source and Ethereum as the destination.

> Withdrawals from Arbitrum One to Ethereum Mainnet through the official bridge have a challenge period of about 7 days. After it ends, you must submit a final claim transaction on Ethereum Mainnet, which costs ETH gas.

## Bridging FAQs

### Do I need to bridge GRT to use The Graph?

Only if your GRT is on Ethereum Mainnet. Delegating, staking, curating, and paying for queries all happen on Arbitrum One.

### Can I pay for Subgraph Studio queries with GRT on Ethereum?

Yes. Subgraph Studio accepts GRT from Ethereum Mainnet as a deposit and bridges it to Arbitrum One for you. Your billing balance itself is always held on Arbitrum One, and withdrawals from it are sent to your wallet on Arbitrum One.

### Why can't I see my GRT after bridging?

Make sure your wallet is connected to Arbitrum One and that you've added the Arbitrum One GRT token contract (`0x9623063377AD1B27544C965cCd7342f7EA7e88C7`). If the transaction is recent, give the bridge time to finish.
