### Indexing Data on Hemi

Hemi is a modular Layer 2 that combines Bitcoin and Ethereum into a single supernetwork, exposing Bitcoin state to EVM smart contracts through its hVM, with ETH for gas. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Hemi is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — develop, test, and publish them in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/using-subgraph-studio/). Publishing is enabled, though guaranteed indexing via The Graph Network is not yet available for Hemi.
