### Indexing Data on Etherlink

Etherlink is an EVM-compatible Layer 2 built on Tezos Smart Rollups, offering low fees and fast confirmations with XTZ for gas. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Etherlink is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — develop, test, and publish them in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/using-subgraph-studio/). Publishing is enabled, though guaranteed indexing via The Graph Network is not yet available for Etherlink.
