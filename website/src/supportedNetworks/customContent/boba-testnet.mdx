### Indexing Data on Boba Sepolia Testnet

Boba Sepolia Testnet is the public test network for Boba Network, an Ethereum Layer 2 optimistic rollup, where teams deploy and validate contracts before mainnet. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Boba Sepolia Testnet is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — open APIs you publish to The Graph Network, where Indexers sync and serve the data while you manage your service in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/providers/subgraph-studio/introduction/).
