### Indexing Data on Japan Open Chain

Japan Open Chain is an EVM-compatible Layer 1 operated by trusted Japanese enterprises and designed for compliant, business-grade applications, with JOC as its native token. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Japan Open Chain is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — develop, test, and publish them in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/using-subgraph-studio/). Publishing is enabled, though guaranteed indexing via The Graph Network is not yet available for Japan Open Chain.
