## Indexing Data on BNB Smart Chain Chapel Testnet

BNB Smart Chain Chapel Testnet is the public test network for BNB Smart Chain, the EVM-compatible Layer 1, where teams deploy and validate contracts before mainnet. The Graph can index that onchain activity, including DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events. That powers DeFi frontends, analytics dashboards, block explorers, portfolio trackers, and onchain agents without teams having to run their own indexing infrastructure.

BNB Smart Chain Chapel Testnet is supported by The Graph in two ways:

- **[Subgraphs](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/publishing-a-subgraph/)**: The blockchain indexing standard perfect for small to medium-sized projects that need hosted APIs. Data consumers manage their service in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/providers/subgraph-studio/introduction/) while Indexers on The Graph Network sync and serve queries.
- **[Substreams](/substreams/quick-start/)**: A real-time streaming engine for transaction-heavy projects, high-throughput chains, and institutions that need custom data infrastructure. Data consumers can manage their service and get indexing via [Pinax Network](https://app.pinax.network/docs?from=nav).
