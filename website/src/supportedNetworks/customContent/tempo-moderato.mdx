### Indexing Data on Tempo Moderato Testnet

Tempo Moderato Testnet is the public test network for Tempo, the Stripe- and Paradigm-built Layer 1 for stablecoin payments, where teams deploy and validate contracts before mainnet. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Tempo Moderato Testnet is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — develop, test, and publish them in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/using-subgraph-studio/). Publishing is enabled, though guaranteed indexing via The Graph Network is not yet available for Tempo Moderato Testnet.
