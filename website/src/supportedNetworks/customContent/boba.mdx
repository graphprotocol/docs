### Indexing Data on Boba

Boba Network is an Ethereum Layer 2 optimistic rollup that adds hybrid compute for calling external APIs from smart contracts, supporting DeFi and consumer applications. The Graph can index that onchain activity — DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events — and serve it over GraphQL with Subgraphs.

Boba is supported by The Graph with Subgraphs — open APIs you publish to The Graph Network, where Indexers sync and serve the data while you manage your service in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/providers/subgraph-studio/introduction/).
