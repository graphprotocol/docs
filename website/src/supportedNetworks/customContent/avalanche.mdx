## Indexing Data on Avalanche

Avalanche C-Chain is the EVM-compatible contract chain of the Avalanche network, a high-throughput Layer 1 with sub-second finality and a broad DeFi, gaming, and institutional ecosystem, with AVAX as its native token. The Graph can index that onchain activity, including DEX swaps, pools, and liquidity; lending and borrowing positions; token transfers and holder balances; and contract events. That powers DeFi frontends, analytics dashboards, block explorers, portfolio trackers, and onchain agents without teams having to run their own indexing infrastructure.

Avalanche is supported by The Graph in two ways:

- **[Subgraphs](/subgraphs/developing/deploying-publishing/publishing-a-subgraph/)**: The blockchain indexing standard perfect for small to medium-sized projects that need hosted APIs. Data consumers manage their service in [Subgraph Studio](/subgraphs/providers/subgraph-studio/introduction/) while Indexers on The Graph Network sync and serve queries.
- **[Substreams](/substreams/quick-start/)**: A real-time streaming engine for transaction-heavy projects, high-throughput chains, and institutions that need custom data infrastructure. Data consumers can manage their service and get indexing via [The Graph Market](https://thegraph.market/).
